# Run folders share through links

A `run` session works like any other, because its folder links to Claude Code's folder for what does not belong to one account: projects with their memory, skills, plugins, history and more.
The full lists are `shared_dirs` and `shared_files` in `clacc`.

Something a session made in the run folder before Claude Code's folder had it moves over and becomes a link.
Something that exists on both sides stays where it is, and clacc says so at every start, so that a person can remove one of the two.
